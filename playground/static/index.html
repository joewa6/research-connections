About this dataset

This is a working prototype with 1,000 fictional researchers, 1,500 projects and 3,000 researcher–project links. Each person has 1–5 projects.

Profiles include their research, expertise, support needs and desired outcomes. Shared projects connect people across four sustainability research areas.

The themes are inspired by IIT's SUSTech programme. This independent prototype is not affiliated with IIT and does not describe its researchers or projects.

Search uses literal text. Collaborator suggestions match expertise tags; they do not assess availability or scientific suitability.

Built with SQLite and Python. The dataset stays read-only.
